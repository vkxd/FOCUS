## ✨ Features

- **🎯 Focus Timer:** Stay locked into your deep-work sessions with a precise visual countdown ring and large digital clock display.
- **⏯️ Full Playback Controls:** Easily start, pause, reset, and skip through your timer intervals.
- **📋 Dynamic Checklist:** Manage your tasks in real-time. Add new items, monitor your completion status (`0/2`), and cross things off as you achieve them.
- **🌙 Modern Dark UI:** Crafted with a clean, high-contrast dark theme designed to reduce eye strain and eliminate workflow distractions.
